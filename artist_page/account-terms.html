Account terms · version 2026-11-18

Your ARC account

  • Keep your own sign-in details private. Usernames are unique without regard to capitalization.
  • A free account lasts seven days from creation. At expiry, its account details, password hash, profile, and sessions are deleted from the active account database. Signing in does not restart the seven days. Database backups may retain older copies until their retention period ends.
  • A verified paid membership preserves your profile long term. Continued sign-in requires an account in good standing. If a paid period ends, the profile remains stored but paid features stop until membership is renewed.
  • Selecting paid membership is not payment and does not activate paid features. Weekly/monthly payment is separate; the price and period are shown before checkout. The current checkout does not automatically renew or charge again.
  • Artists require active paid membership to publish and participate in member transactions.
  • Visitors and free accounts can view content artists mark free. They cannot purchase, transfer, or trade ARC coins.
  • Fans buy Lion and Crown from ARC. Artists buy Bird and Bee inventory and trade it with an agreed content or interaction benefit. The artist's received Lion and Crown is eligible for a conversion request at half its original value. Actual payout is separate from requesting conversion.
  • The fan's received Bird and Bee is non-refundable, non-cash-convertible store credit worth half the original Lion and Crown value used in the trade. It can support artists' selected gifts/rewards at the platform's published venue values, including partial contributions pooled with other fans.
  • Spent Bird and Bee returns to ARC for recirculation. A fully funded gift still requires actual platform fulfillment; recording funding is not proof of delivery. Published production prices and final conversion/dispute agreements govern live transactions; simulation examples are not published prices.

Acceptance of this version is recorded with the account. These are account-access terms, not the future currency conversion agreement.
