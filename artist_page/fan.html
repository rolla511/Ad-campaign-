Lion and Crown · Bird and Bee

Fans buy Lion and Crown from ARC. Artists buy Bird and Bee inventory and offer it with their content or interactions. After the trade, Bird and Bee is non-refundable gift/reward store credit worth half the original Lion and Crown value.

Platform gifts use the venue value shown in the catalog. Spent Bird and Bee returns to ARC for recirculation.
